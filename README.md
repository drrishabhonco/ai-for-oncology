# AI for Oncology

Practical AI workflows for oncology education, literature synthesis, clinical communication, and cancer-care systems.

## Vision

Artificial intelligence will not replace oncologists.

But oncologists who learn to build better AI-assisted workflows may significantly improve:
- education
- literature synthesis
- patient communication
- clinical efficiency
- multidisciplinary collaboration

This repository explores practical and responsible applications of AI in oncology.

## Focus Areas

- Oncology literature summarization
- AI-assisted educational workflows
- Patient education systems
- Clinical communication tools
- AI safety in oncology
- Workflow automation
- Precision oncology workflows
- Responsible use of large language models (LLMs)

## Repository Structure

### `/trial-summarization`
Frameworks for extracting key insights from oncology trials.

### `/patient-education`
Simplified multilingual oncology communication templates.

### `/clinical-ai-safety`
Checklists and frameworks for identifying hallucinations and unsafe outputs.

### `/oncology-prompts-public`
Selected public prompts for oncology education and workflow support.

## Important Disclaimer

These tools are intended for:
- education
- workflow support
- research exploration

They are NOT:
- clinical decision-making systems
- substitutes for guidelines
- replacements for physician judgment

Clinical decisions should always follow:
- institutional protocols
- peer-reviewed evidence
- multidisciplinary discussion
- physician expertise
